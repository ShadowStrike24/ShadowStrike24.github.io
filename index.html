eFootball Mobile: Ultimate Starter Guide & Pro Tactics

Mastering build-up play and defensive positioning is key to dominating the pitch in eFootball Mobile. Here are the essential strategies every player needs to know:

1. Tactical Synergy & Manager Proficiency

Ensure your squad matches your manager's preferred playstyle (Quick Counter or Possession Game). Boosting team playstyle proficiency above 85 unlocks significant stat boosts for your players.

2. Mastering Defensive Timing

Avoid reckless tackles. Instead, hold down the match-up button to intercept passing lanes and force opponents into low-probability shots.

3. Efficient Build-Up Play

Use short passes in midfield to draw out defenders before launching through-balls to high-speed wingers.
